Our Story

Three generations on the grill.

Kebab House Bolton opened its doors in 1976 and quickly built a name for itself with a homemade red donner recipe that customers still ask for by name. In 1983, Mohammed Fazal and his two sons took over the business, and today a third generation of the family keeps the grills running.

Nothing here is mass-produced. Meat is marinated and prepped in-house, bread is baked fresh, and the menu has grown over the decades from regulars telling us what they wanted more of. We're a collection-only takeaway — no delivery platforms, no middlemen — just food made to order and handed straight to you.
